About Ganga Aarti

The Ganga Aarti is a sacred Hindu ritual performed daily on the banks of the River Ganges in Varanasi. This mesmerizing ceremony involves the offering of light to the goddess Ganga, performed with grace and devotion. Thousands of pilgrims and visitors gather to witness this spiritual spectacle at sunset, experiencing the divine energy that flows through the sacred waters of the Ganges.
